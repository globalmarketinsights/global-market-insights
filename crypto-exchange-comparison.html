Independent research guide

How to compare crypto exchanges

A practical checklist for evaluating an exchange before you create an account or deposit money. The right questions matter more than a flashy promotion or a high commission offer.

Use the comparison checklist ↗Back to Crypto Hub

For readers in Nigeria, the United States and other markets. Eligibility depends on your actual country of residence and the exact service or legal entity involved.

01 · First check

Is the exchange legally available where you live?

Do not assume an exchange accepts customers in every country. An exchange may operate in one country but restrict another, or offer spot trading while limiting other products. Its affiliate programme may also have different rules from its customer services.

Important: Never enter a false country of residence, use a VPN to bypass restrictions or apply with someone else's identity. If the provider does not clearly confirm that your country and intended service are supported, do not deposit.
  • Find the exact legal company that would serve your account.
  • Check the provider's current restricted-country and product-availability pages.
  • For the United States, verify state-level restrictions as well as federal eligibility.
  • For Nigeria, check local access, supported payment methods and withdrawal options.
  • Check the relevant regulator's official register where applicable. A registration in one country is not worldwide approval.
02 · Compare carefully

Exchange comparison checklist

Use this table to compare providers using information from their official terms and fee pages. Fill it in for your own country; do not rely on old reviews or social-media claims.

Country and account eligibility

Does the provider accept residents of your country and your state or region? Which legal entity provides the account?

Trading and conversion fees

Check maker/taker fees, spreads, instant-buy charges, currency conversion and any minimum transaction amounts.

Deposits and withdrawals

Check available payment methods, supported currencies, bank or mobile-money options, withdrawal fees, limits and processing times.

Security and account protection

Look for passkeys or two-factor authentication, withdrawal address controls, account alerts and clear incident communication. No security feature removes all risk.

Asset and network support

Confirm that the exact asset and blockchain network you intend to use are supported for both deposits and withdrawals. Similar token names do not mean networks are interchangeable.

Customer support and complaints

Check support channels, response options, dispute procedures and whether the provider explains how customer complaints are handled.

Affiliate or promotional terms

Promotional rewards can have conditions, exclusions and expiry dates. Read the terms; do not choose an exchange just because a referral offer looks attractive.

04 · Protect your money

Before you deposit

  • Start by learning how the platform works; do not rush because of a bonus countdown.
  • Use a unique password and enable strong two-factor authentication.
  • Never share your password, one-time code, private key or wallet recovery phrase.
  • Understand whether you are buying crypto, using a custodial service or trading a derivative.
  • Test the provider's official withdrawal process and understand all fees before committing a meaningful amount.
  • Be cautious of anyone claiming they can recover lost crypto for an upfront payment.
