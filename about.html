About this project

Knowledge for a global audience

Global Market Insights is an independent educational website focused on explaining crypto, forex and financial-market topics in accessible language.

Our purpose

Our goal is to help readers understand financial terminology, ask better questions and recognise common risks. We aim to make complex topics easier to approach without promising profits or presenting speculation as certainty.

Editorial principles

  • Explain concepts in plain language.
  • Separate education from personal financial recommendations.
  • Be transparent about commercial relationships and affiliate links.
  • Correct errors when they are identified.
  • Encourage readers to verify important claims with primary sources and relevant regulators.

Who this is for

Readers around the world who want a starting point for learning about digital assets, currency markets and financial literacy. Content is reviewed for accuracy and local regulatory relevance.
