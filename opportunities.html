Offers with clear conditions

Opportunities

A place for legitimate educational resources, partner offers and services that may be relevant to readers. We will list an offer only when its terms, eligibility and commercial relationship can be explained clearly.

What we may feature

  • Free educational resources and learning events.
  • Approved affiliate programmes with clearly disclosed qualifying actions.
  • Services that are available to the stated country or audience.
  • Occasional discounts or promotions with visible expiry dates and conditions.

What we will not promise

We will not describe a deposit bonus, trading promotion or referral offer as free money. We will not publish guaranteed-return claims, conceal eligibility restrictions or encourage readers to trade to earn us a commission.

Check before taking part

Read the official offer terms, check the exact provider and legal entity, confirm your country is eligible, understand fees and withdrawal rules, and consider whether the service is appropriate for you. Offers can change or end without notice.
