01 · Start here

What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a digital asset that uses cryptography and, in many cases, a blockchain or similar distributed ledger to record transfers. Different crypto projects have different designs, purposes and risks. Owning a cryptocurrency does not mean owning a share in a company or having a guaranteed claim on income.

Before using a crypto service, learn how the asset works, how you would store it, what fees apply and what rules apply where you live.

03 · Compare the categories

Not all crypto assets do the same job

Payment-focused assets

Some networks are designed to transfer value. Consider network fees, confirmation times, adoption and volatility.

Smart-contract platforms

These networks support applications and programmable transactions. Consider network activity, fees, security and ecosystem risks.

Stablecoins

These tokens aim to maintain a value linked to another asset. They still carry issuer, reserve, redemption and technology risks.

These are broad educational categories, not rankings or recommendations. A project's label does not prove that it is safe or suitable.

04 · Research checklist

Questions to ask before trusting a crypto project

  • Purpose: What problem does it claim to solve, and can you explain how it works?
  • People and governance: Who maintains it, and how are important decisions made?
  • Token design: What is the supply, who holds it and when can more tokens enter circulation?
  • Security: Are audits and incident histories available? Remember, an audit cannot remove all risk.
  • Liquidity and costs: Can you understand the spread, network fee, withdrawal fee and liquidity?
  • Independent evidence: Can you verify claims from sources beyond promotional posts?
05 · Protect yourself

Crypto safety essentials

Keep recovery phrases private

Never share your seed phrase or private key. Genuine support staff should not ask you to reveal them.

Check every address

Verify the network and destination address carefully. A transfer sent to the wrong address or network may be irreversible.

Be suspicious of guarantees

Guaranteed profits, urgent pressure, celebrity giveaways and requests to pay extra money to unlock withdrawals are major warning signs.

Secure your accounts

Use unique passwords, enable strong two-factor authentication and protect the email account linked to your exchange.

Risk reminder: Crypto prices can rise or fall sharply. You could lose some or all of the money you put at risk. Other risks include scams, exchange failures, software vulnerabilities, lost keys, liquidity problems and changing regulations. This page is general education, not personal financial advice or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any asset.
