Crypto education

Understand cryptocurrency

Learn the concepts behind digital assets before making decisions or using any crypto service.

What is cryptocurrency?

Cryptocurrency is a type of digital asset that can be transferred and recorded using a blockchain or another cryptographic system. Some networks allow people to send value without relying on a traditional bank to maintain the transaction ledger.

Bitcoin and Ethereum are two well-known examples, but crypto assets differ widely in purpose, design, governance and risk.

What is a blockchain?

A blockchain is a shared digital record maintained by a network of computers. Transactions are grouped into blocks and linked in sequence. Depending on the network, participants use consensus rules to agree on which transactions are valid.

Wallets and private keys

A crypto wallet helps you manage the keys used to access digital assets. A public address can be shared to receive funds. A private key or recovery phrase must be kept secret: anyone who obtains it may be able to control the associated assets. Legitimate support staff should never ask you to reveal your recovery phrase.

How to research a crypto project

  • Read the project's official documentation and understand its actual use case.
  • Check who maintains the project and whether claims can be independently verified.
  • Understand token supply, unlock schedules, liquidity and governance.
  • Look for independent security audits, while remembering that audits do not eliminate risk.
  • Be cautious of guaranteed returns, pressure to recruit others and unsolicited investment messages.

Common crypto risks

Prices can be extremely volatile. Other risks include scams, exchange failures, software vulnerabilities, lost keys, liquidity problems and changing regulations. Never invest money you cannot afford to lose.

This page is general education, not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any cryptocurrency.
