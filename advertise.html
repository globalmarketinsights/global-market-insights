Commercial partnerships

Advertising & Partnerships

Information for financial businesses interested in reaching readers of Global Market Insights. Any future commercial placement will be clearly labelled and reviewed before publication.

Current status: We are not currently offering paid advertising inventory or accepting paid placements. This page explains our standards for future enquiries.

Sponsored placements

When the website has a suitable audience, we may consider clearly labelled sponsored articles, display placements or educational campaigns from legitimate businesses. Paid placement will never be presented as independent editorial coverage or a guaranteed endorsement.

  • All sponsored material will be labelled clearly as advertising or sponsored content.
  • We will identify the advertiser and explain relevant commercial relationships.
  • We will reject misleading claims, guaranteed-return promotions, impersonation, scams and unsuitable products.
  • We reserve editorial control and may decline or remove a placement.

Responsible lead-generation partnerships

We may consider referring interested readers to eligible services where this is lawful and appropriate. A qualified lead must be a genuine enquiry, not a fabricated or purchased contact. Before any lead programme is activated, we will review the provider, country eligibility, permitted data fields, consent wording, privacy obligations, retention and deletion arrangements, and how the lead is used.

We do not currently operate a lead-collection form or sell or share visitor contact details for lead generation. No reader information will be sent to a partner without an appropriate explanation and valid consent where required.

Affiliate and editorial independence

Affiliate partnerships are separate from sponsorships. If a page contains an affiliate link, we will disclose that relationship near the link. Payment does not guarantee a favourable review, ranking or recommendation. See our Partners, Reviews & Comparisons and Financial Disclaimer pages.

Enquiries

Businesses may send a brief introduction, official website, legal entity name, countries served, product category and proposed collaboration. An enquiry does not mean a partnership is accepted.
