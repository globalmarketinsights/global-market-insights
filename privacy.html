Our commitment

We respect your privacy. This website does not require visitors to create an account. We receive personal information only when you choose to contact us or subscribe to our newsletter through our external email service.

Information you provide

Our newsletter signup links to Kit (ConvertKit), an external email marketing service. If you subscribe, the information you submit is processed by Kit to manage the subscription and send emails on our behalf, subject to Kit’s own privacy terms. The calculators run in your browser using the figures you enter; calculator inputs are not sent to a calculator server by this website.

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details and request logs under its own privacy practices. We do not currently use third-party advertising trackers or analytics on this website. We do not currently operate lead-generation forms, sell visitor contact details or share newsletter subscriber information with affiliate or advertising partners. If this changes, we will update this notice and provide appropriate information and choices before collecting or sharing information for that purpose.
