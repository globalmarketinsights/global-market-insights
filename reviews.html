Reviews & Comparisons

A home for evidence-led educational reviews of crypto exchanges, forex brokers, wallets and useful financial tools. We will publish provider-specific reviews only after checking current information and clearly stating limitations.

Our review checklist

Regulation & legal entity

Identify the exact contracting company and verify its status with relevant official registers.

Country eligibility

Check whether the provider accepts customers in the reader's country and which entity serves them.

Fees & conditions

Compare spreads, commissions, deposit and withdrawal charges, conversion costs and account rules.

Security & custody

Explain account safeguards, custody arrangements, wallet controls and recovery limitations.

Usability & support

Consider platform access, documentation, customer support and available learning resources.

Risks & complaints

Highlight material risks, restrictions and credible concerns rather than hiding drawbacks.

Current published comparison

Our crypto exchange comparison guide explains what readers should verify before choosing an exchange. It is an educational checklist, not a claim that every provider is available in every country.

For forex, start with our trading costs guide, leverage and margin guide and risk management guide.

Commercial transparency

Some reviews may contain affiliate links if a programme approves our participation. Any such relationship will be disclosed near the link. Compensation will not buy a positive rating, and we will not describe an untested provider as independently verified.

A provider review is not a recommendation to open an account or trade. Financial products may be restricted by country and can involve substantial risk.
