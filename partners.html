How we assess providers

  • Regulatory status: identify the legal entity and check relevant regulator registers where applicable. A licence in one country does not automatically authorise a provider everywhere.
  • Country availability: check whether the service accepts customers in the reader's location, including Nigeria where relevant.
  • Costs and conditions: review spreads, commissions, deposit and withdrawal fees, conversion charges, minimums and account conditions.
  • Security and custody: explain how accounts, customer funds, crypto assets and recovery processes are handled, without implying that risk can be eliminated.
  • Complaints and limitations: look for material restrictions, unresolved concerns and risks that readers should understand before signing up.

Important safety checks before signing up

  • Visit the provider's official website by typing its address yourself; beware of sponsored impersonation links and messages.
  • Check the exact company name and legal entity with the appropriate regulator in your country.
  • Read current fees, withdrawal rules, customer agreements and risk warnings before depositing.
  • Never share a password, one-time code, private key or wallet recovery phrase with anyone.
  • Be wary of guaranteed profits, pressure to deposit quickly, unsolicited account managers and requests to pay extra money to release supposed profits.
Crypto assets and leveraged forex products involve substantial risk. You can lose some or all of the money you commit. This website provides general education, not personal financial, investment or legal advice. We do not provide trading signals or promise returns.
